Every MD-11 panel control now tells you its state: "Battery: On", "External Power: Available",
"Generator 1: Armed", "Pack 1: Off", and "unpowered" when the aircraft is cold and dark, with a
spoken confirmation after each press. Panels are ordered the way you prepare the aircraft
(Electrical first on the overhead, then IRS, Fuel, Hydraulic, Air…), guard covers sit right before
the switch they protect, and the labels no longer collide or read as gibberish.

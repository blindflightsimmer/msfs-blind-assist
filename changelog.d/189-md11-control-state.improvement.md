Every MD-11 panel control now tells you its state: "Battery: On", "External Power: Available",
"Generator 1: Armed", "Pack 1: Off", and "unpowered" whenever the annunciators have no power —
cold and dark, and on the battery alone, where the lights go out without the systems being off —
with a spoken confirmation after each press. Panels are ordered the way you prepare the aircraft
(Electrical first on the overhead, then IRS, Fuel, Hydraulic, Air…), guard covers sit right before
the switch they protect, and the labels no longer collide or read as gibberish.

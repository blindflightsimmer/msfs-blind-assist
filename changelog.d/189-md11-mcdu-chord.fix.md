The MD-11 hotkey guide told you to press Shift+M for the MCDU and Shift+T for the EFB. Both need the left bracket first, to put the app into input mode — pressed on their own they do nothing, and in output mode Shift+M reads the METAR instead. A pilot following the guide could conclude the MCDU window was missing when it was working the whole time. The guide now names the full keystroke.

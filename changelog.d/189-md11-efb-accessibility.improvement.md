The MD-11's EFB window now reads like the other tablets. Every Options setting is a dropdown that says its current value ("Weight Units: Imperial") and the section tabs say which section is open. On the Perf page, Runway, Runway Condition, Autobrake, Reversers, Thrust Setting and Anti-Ice are dropdowns listing every choice, fields carry their unit ("Temperature (°C)"), and results read as one line ("Estimated Landing Distance: 7123 ft", "V1: 155"). Doors and ground equipment say what they act on ("GPU: Connect", "Passenger 1L: Closed"), the State page says which panel state is the default, the Dispatch header is labelled (flight, aircraft, from, to, times, alternate, route) and the OFP is read line by line. The EFB's own pop-up messages ("Options saved successfully.") are spoken, and on pages the EFB locks in flight the controls read as dimmed instead of silently doing nothing.

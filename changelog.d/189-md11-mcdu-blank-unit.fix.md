The MD-11 MCDU window opens on the Left unit. When that unit had nothing on its screen the window showed eight empty rows — "Title:", "1:" through "6:", "Scratchpad:" — over a status line reading "MCDU: connected", and said nothing at all. There was no way to tell a blank CDU from a broken window, and a working MCDU feed got reported as the window not showing up. A unit with nothing on it now says so, and names the other units that do have a page and the keystroke to reach them.

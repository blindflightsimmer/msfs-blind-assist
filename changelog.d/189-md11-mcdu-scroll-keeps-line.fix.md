MD-11 MCDU window: scrolling the flight plan with Alt+Up or Alt+Down no longer throws your cursor off the line you were reading. Two things did that: the window put the cursor back by row number, but a page with fewer labels has fewer rows, so line 6 moved; and the MD-11 keeps its page counter in the title ("ACT F-PLN 1/2"), so crossing a page boundary looked like a new page and sent the cursor to line 1. The cursor now stays on the line it was on, and only a genuinely different page moves it to line 1. The new page title is still announced when it changes.

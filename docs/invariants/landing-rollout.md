# Landing rollout guidance — rules in full

Each section is the complete text of one rule. Its one-line form, under the same ID, is in `.claude/rules/landing-rollout.md`, which Claude Code loads when it reads matching code. Background: [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md).
The text is verbatim from CLAUDE.md as of `1f37801a`; a trailing "→ doc" pointer is the original's. Cross-references such as "the bullet below", "above" or "under Core" point at CLAUDE.md's old single list, whose rules now live in several files: search `docs/invariants/` for the rule's key name to find it.
ROL-30 onward came with the taxi/landing port (`feat/taxi-landing-port`) and are verbatim from that branch's CLAUDE.md as of `4854ec3d`. They sit on top of main's own designs, which win wherever the two overlap; their figures were measured on the owner's local build, and of the sweeps they name only `tools/VirtualPilot` and `tools/ExitConsistencySweep` are in the repository, so re-measure with VirtualPilot ([virtual-pilot.md](../virtual-pilot.md)) before touching one.

## ROL-1

- The manual landing assist hands its rollout tone to taxi guidance SILENTLY on the frame taxi guidance leaves a landing rollout for anything but a route reload or a stop (`LandingFlareAssistManager.StepTaxiHandover`, fed from `StateChanged` and yielded after the taxi position update) — never by widening `IsLandingExitTaxiSteering` to count backtracking, which ended the two-tone overlap but let the assist's interrupting "Rollout guidance complete" cut "End of runway … Turn around" off within a frame. A Taxi Stop is not a takeover (the assist is independent of taxi guidance), and the assist's own speed or turn end is QUEUED whenever taxi guidance ran during the rollout, so it never clips a countdown, "Runway vacated…" or closure sentence. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## ROL-2

- Rollout handoff to Taxiing must require `turnBegun || (atTaxiSpeed && nearExit)` — do NOT relax `nearExit` back to a speed-only condition; a long runway drops below 30kt thousands of feet before the planned exit. `turnBegun` itself is not just the heading/speed pair below — see the SIGNED/proximity-gated bullet further down this group. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## ROL-3

- Never remove the `ROLLOUT_TURN_MAX_GS_KTS` (90kt) speed cap on `turnBegun` — above 90kt a heading deviation is touchdown yaw/crosswind crab, not a real exit turn (KJFK 22L false-trigger). → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## ROL-4

- `EnterRunwayEndCountdown` must null `_route`/`_destinationNodeId` on overshoot-with-no-exit, or `TryRecalculateRoute` routes back across the runway to the already-passed exit.

## ROL-5

- The overshoot "downfield" cutoff must be measured from the AIRCRAFT as well as the missed exit (`RolloutExitGate.DownfieldCutoffFeet`) — a high-speed exit is not declared missed until up to `ROLLOUT_HIGHSPEED_OVERSHOOT_FT` (500 ft) past it, so an exit-relative cutoff calls a turnoff 200 ft beyond the exit "downfield" while it sits 250 ft BEHIND the wing, and the retarget pans the tone back at it. The aircraft is a FLOOR, never an extra margin on top of the exit-relative value — stacking them tightens the tuned normal-exit path for no safety gain. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## ROL-6

- Every path that lands in `Taxiing` with a null `_route` must STOP the steering tone first — `UpdatePosition` early-returns before touching the tone in that state, so a tone left sounding holds its last pan forever (CYYZ: the backtrack handoff's "Runway vacated." branch left it panned hard right for 68 s; its sibling no-connection branch had always stopped it). Say what happened too: a status query there answers "No route loaded.", which is true but reads as a fault unless the pilot was told. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## ROL-7

- A go-around or touch-and-go after touchdown ENDS landing-exit guidance and keeps the plan (`Services/LandingExitGoAround`): while KNOWN airborne the rollout is held (`HoldsRollout`, unknown counts as the ground, never the other way); MainForm arms a one-shot check on the liftoff edge while landing-exit guidance runs (`Arms`: `LandingRollout` or landing-exit `Taxiing`), a touchdown stops it as a bounce, and after `ConfirmMs` (5 s) a FRESH position read decides (`Ends`), never the 1 Hz cache — the liftoff handoff's pattern, token-guarded against a response landing after a touchdown, disconnect or aircraft switch. It stops guidance as `StopGuidance` does, re-arms the planner (`RearmAfterGoAround`) and speaks ONE sentence ("Exit guidance off, plan kept."). Before it, nothing ended the rollout at liftoff: exit callouts spoke into the climb-out and the next approach had no exit guidance. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## ROL-8

- `TryEarlyExitHandoff` must fire ONLY for High-speed exits (angle <50°) — never restore it for Normal/End exits; it caused a 90°-exit tone to hard-pan 300ft early with no verbal cue (EGNX miss). → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## ROL-9

- Every handoff to Taxiing must always re-route from the live aircraft position via the extension-node logic — never revert to the ApronNodeId-only re-route (Normal/End exits have ApronNodeId==NodeId and would keep the bogus initial route). → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## ROL-10

- The post-high-speed-exit `ExitBearingTrue` pan floor has exactly TWO sanctioned releases — the opposite-SIGN test while still on the pavement, and an unconditional release once the aircraft is laterally CLEAR of the runway (`IsWithinRolloutRunwayLaterally`) — never magnitude-vs-floor, and never restore the unconditional `Math.Max/Min` clamp: magnitude gating reintroduces the wrong-side hard-pan reversal seen at CYVR, while the lateral gate is a position test that cannot (the distortion the floor bridges only exists on the pavement, and the CYVR case is still caught earlier by the sign test). → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## ROL-11

- Both override guards (apron forward-direction AND `apronAngle > currentAngleFwd`) are required together in the implicit-exit shallow-angle override — dropping either regresses the exit bearing. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## ROL-12

- The `exitedLaterally` handoff trigger must use the combined gate (lateral + dist/hdgDelta/pastExit), never bare lateral distance — bare lateral fires too eagerly before the tone's exit-bearing phase engages (EDDB false trigger); that window is the audible `DriftCorrection` tone, not silent. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## ROL-13

- The passive-handoff `exitedLaterallyPH` check must NOT be gated the same way as the trigger — different semantics; gating it delays clearing the overshoot monitor. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## ROL-14

- `_rolloutRunway` must stay cached through `EnterRunwayEndCountdown` — the countdown needs it; full silence on a missed-last-exit is unsafe for a blind pilot. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## ROL-15

- `BeginLandingRolloutNoGraph` must defensively null `_route` at entry regardless of the takeoff path, so the handoff-failure fallback invariant always holds. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## ROL-16

- `RetargetLandingExit` must cascade through every downfield exit before giving up — one bad `LoadRoute` on the first candidate must not skip good later exits (YSSY case). The caller's reason holds for every candidate, and an EARLIER-exit retarget's fall-forward stops SILENTLY at the exit already targeted (`RetargetCallout.StaysOnPlannedExit`) with the `LoadRoute` rollback restored — it fell onto that same exit as "Missed taxiway P. Retargeting taxiway P" about an exit still ahead, every 8 s — and an exit that fails to route is remembered for the rollout and skipped by the undershoot scan. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## ROL-17

- The undershoot retarget scan needs a speed-proportional minimum lead distance, not "nearest within 1000ft" — the naive version retargeted to an exit impossible to make at the aircraft's speed. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## ROL-18

- The rollout announce latches (e.g. `_rolloutApproach900Announced`) must be reset at all four reset sites (`BeginLandingRollout`, `BeginLandingRolloutNoGraph`, `EnterRunwayEndCountdown`, `StopGuidance`). → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## ROL-19

- **THE DERIVED-CONSTANT TRIPWIRE — re-derive all five before changing any tolerance in the runway/rollout area, and say so in the commit message.** `RolloutExitGate.VacatedShortAlongTrackFeet` (350), `EarlyVacateMaxPassedFeet` (1400) and the 25 m corridor clamp (`HandoffReachDefaultHalfWidthM`) are all arithmetic consequences of the exact **5 m** gap between the exit-node corridor (`halfWidth + HandoffReachMarginM`, 15 m) and the pavement boundary (`halfWidth + RunwayClearMarginM`, 10 m) — `halfWidth` cancels; `RunwayClearMarginM` (10) is the codebase's ONE definition of "off the runway"; `DefaultRunwayWidthFeet` (200) is a fallback half-width DIFFERENT from `RunwayShape.DefaultHalfWidthMeters` (75 ft). Nothing in the code, the compiler or the tests links them, there is no compile error when they stop being derived, and the boundary tests keep passing because they pin the OLD arithmetic. `RunwayVacateResolver` keeps its own 75 ft copy and its own `SameRunwayLateralM` (30.0), calibrated against the residual scatter `TaxiGraph.SnapStartToRunwayCenterline` leaves — so loosening the snap invalidates that too. Since 2026-09 the same constants also drive the landing-exit branch measurement and the off-pavement alert: `RunwayClearMarginM` is `ExitBranch`'s clear line (every measured branch ends there, so it decides every measured exit's angle), `PavementMap` reads `RunwayClearMarginM` for a runway (its taxiway reach is `PavementTolerance.ForWidthFeet`, the off-route detector's own), and `TurnWindowFeetFor` falls back to `DefaultRunwayWidthFeet` and is floored at `TurnNowFeet` (150 ft); `RunwayAxis.CorridorMarginMetres` IS `HandoffReachMarginM` (linked 2026-09-26, no longer a copy). A change there also moves exit angles, the off-pavement line and the turn window: re-run `tools/LandingExitSweep` too. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## ROL-20

- The rollout exit-turn gate must stay SIGNED (deviation on the exit's own side) and proximity-gated (within the exit's own turn window — `RolloutExitGate.TurnWindowFeetFor`, never more than 1,000 ft — or past it) — the bare `Math.Abs(hdgDelta) >= 15` form read a 15° LEFT drift 2,232 ft short of a RIGHT-hand exit as the exit turn (KSEA 34L). → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## ROL-21

- The rollout steering tone has THREE modes (silent >50 kt / exit-bearing ≤300 ft / drift-correction in between — which steers to the runway HEADING, a heading cue with no cross-track term, so never describe it as steering back to the centreline) — never restore the two-mode "exit-only" design that left the middle of the rollout with no cue at all; and within the targeted exit's own turn window (`TurnWindowFeetFor`, capped at `TurnWindowFeet`, 1,000 ft) the drift band ALSO goes silent for a deviation ≥ `DriftToneSilentDeg` (2°) toward a KNOWN exit side, so the tone never fights a turn `IsExitTurnBegun` is about to accept; and while the aircraft is too fast for the targeted exit (`SelectToneMode`'s `tooFastForExit`: `IsTooFastToTurn` before its turn point, or declined there) the tone is drift-correction wherever it would be exit-bearing or turn-window silent, so it never leads toward, nor goes quiet for a turn toward, an exit the pilot cannot make. After "turn now" a Normal exit's exit-bearing tone may steer to `ExitBearingTrue` only when `RolloutExitGate.IsPlausibleExitBearing` accepts it (known, and within 110° of the runway heading) — KMEM M6 carried 127° true on a 359° runway, and the tone demanded that hairpin at 49 kt; otherwise it keeps the bearing to the exit's node. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## ROL-22

- **Each exit has its own turn window** (`RolloutExitGate.TurnWindowFeetFor`): runway half-width / tan 15° + the exit node's own lateral offset / tan(exit angle), never less than the 150 ft "turn now" cue (`TurnNowFeet` — on a runway narrower than about 80 ft the sum fell below it, and a pilot who turned when told was panned back toward the runway heading until the window; 13,287 of 54,132 usable exits) and capped at 1,000 ft — the fixed 1,000 ft was this sum's worst case (200 ft runway, marker 45.5 m off, 15° = 930 ft), the one derivation `TurnWindowFeet` states. `IsExitTurnBegun` and `SelectToneMode`'s turn-window Silent both take it; the rollout computes it where it is read (`RolloutExitTurnWindowFeet()`), from the exit and runway targeted NOW — never a cached copy every assignment of `_rolloutExit` had to refresh. Never go back to the fixed window for a centerline junction: KMEM M7's window is 324 ft, and the fixed 1,000 ft silenced the tone for a leftover 8–15° right turn 631 ft out and accepted it as the M7 turn 483 ft out — the handoff that swung the tone hard left as the aircraft left the runway. `IsVacateAwayFromPlannedExit` keeps the fixed constant. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## ROL-23

- **"Turn now" is never said when too fast** (`RolloutExitGate.IsTooFastToTurn`: faster than `ExitTurnOffSpeedKts` + `TooFastMarginKts`, 10 kt — 30 kt for an exit of 45° or more or of unknown angle, 60 kt below). At the turn point the rollout retargets downfield (`FindTooFastAlternative`: beyond the declined exit AND one the aircraft can slow down for comfortably — the touchdown re-plan's own rule, `LandingExitReplan.IsUsable` with the Comfortable tier, preferring an exit mapped clear of the runway on a list the scan screens first (`LandingExitVacateScreen`) — else at least `ExitLeadFeet` ahead of the aircraft — alone that lead, tuned below 50 kt, cascaded too-fast retargets above 60 kt; the rescue scan as fallback) with "Too fast for taxiway X. Continue to taxiway Y, N feet."; with nothing ahead it says "Taxiway X, too fast to turn. Slow down." and latches `_rolloutTooFastNoExit`: the tone then HOLDS THE RUNWAY HEADING (`SelectToneMode`'s `tooFastForExit`, which already applies before the turn point while `IsTooFastToTurn` holds) and never pans toward that exit, and the two SPEED-driven handoffs (`speedNearExitHandoff`, `TryEarlyExitHandoff`) stay closed so slowing down as told never re-offers it — the handoffs that follow what the pilot does stay open. It keeps its overshoot margin while rolling — a pilot who slowed and turns onto it anyway gets any exit's allowance (a 0 margin pre-empted exactly that turn) — and is overshot the moment the aircraft STOPS (≤ 3 kt) at or past its node (`RolloutExitGate.IsPastExitForOvershoot`). That overshoot asks `FindTooFastAlternative` again at the speed NOW and QUEUES any retarget behind the warning, or runs the runway-end countdown with no "Missed last exit" and its own status spoken on its first frame (the stopped notice, a milestone, or the owed "Runway end in N.", which alone is QUEUED — the node comes 0.7–3.75 s into the 4.39 s "Taxiway X, too fast to turn. Slow down.", and an interrupting status cut that warning off; the milestones, the stopped notice and the turn-around sentence still interrupt) — never left silent on the runway, which the usual 100 ft margin did. A too-fast call never says "Missed": a fall-forward keeps "Too fast for taxiway M6. Continue to taxiway M8, …", and with no reachable exit it says "Too fast for taxiway M6. No reachable exit remaining." `TryEarlyExitHandoff` also waits for a flyable speed. The crossing decline never folds "turn now" into its sentence at a speed the exit cannot be taken at, and says nothing inside the turn point then (`RolloutRunwayReCrossing.PlanDeclineSpeech`), so the too-fast rule speaks; the touchdown correction's fold has the same guard. "Slow down." has ONE line, `RolloutExitGate.SlowDownAboveKts` — the exit's `MaxTurnSpeedKts`, except that an "End" exit keeps the 30 kt line (`TaxiGroundSpeedKts`) because little runway is left after it — used by the 500 ft callout, the touchdown correction, the crossing decline and the retarget sentence. A high-speed exit, which used to hear it at no speed, now hears it above 60 kt (above 30 kt at 45–50°). → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## ROL-24

- **A retarget is ONE utterance** (`Navigation.RetargetCallout` via `AnnounceRetarget`: Missed, TooFast or Earlier): every approach milestone it supersedes is retired first with the MEASURED lead of the sentence actually spoken (`RetargetCallout.LeadSecondsFor`: too fast 13.5 / 11.5 / 11.5 / 9.5 s and missed 13 / 11.5 / 11.5 / 9.5 s with "Straighten." and "Slow down." / one / the other / neither, earlier 9.5 / 8 s — the worst over every spoken distance up to 3,550 ft at System.Speech Rate 0, plus a fifth; one 13 s lead for all of them retired a short sentence's own 500 ft call, due 11 s later), judged twice when the sentence folds "Slow down." (`RetargetCallout.Retire`) — KMEM 36L: the retarget sentence was cut off 65 ms later by a stale "Taxiway M7, 900 feet." at 631 ft. Turn-now is never retired there, and the retired 500 ft call's "Slow down." is folded in. "Straighten." is added (never to an Earlier retarget) per `ShouldStraightenAfterRetarget`: ≥ 5° off the runway heading that the new exit would not accept as its own turn. Re-measure the lead whenever the wording changes; never size it by estimate. Accepted residual: after a Missed OR a TooFast retarget onto a nearby high-speed exit, the early handoff's own advance notice ("In 300 feet, turn … onto taxiway M7.") can cut the sentence off — same exit, same direction, and "Straighten." is spoken first. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## ROL-25

- **"Off pavement."** (`Navigation.PavementMap` + `OffPavementAlert`) runs during `LandingRollout` (including the runway-end countdown) and landing-exit `Taxiing`, never during backtracks or after exit guidance ends (unmapped aprons). Off = ON THE GROUND (`OffPavementAlert.IsOffPavement`, air/ground from `TaxiGuidanceManager.OnGroundProvider`; the rollout survives liftoff, and a go-around's climb-out heard it every 6 s) AND beyond every runway shape + 10 m AND beyond every taxi edge's `PavementTolerance.ForWidthFeet` (the off-route detector's own reach, never a copy) — and off the runway being landed on, which ALWAYS counts as pavement from the runway table (half-width + 10 m laterally, a PAVED runway's width capped at 400 ft as `RunwayShape` caps a malformed row — ZBAT 546 ft; grass, dirt and water fields keep theirs — its length ± 10 m along-track), because the map knows a runway only through a centreline paired from two `start` rows (KDEN 07/25 has no row for 07; the map alone said "Off pavement." about 4 s after touchdown at 122 kt, on the centreline). Spoken after 1 s off while ≥ 5 kt, every 6 s while still off, re-armed after 2 s back on. It speaks through `_announcer.AnnounceImmediate` directly so Ctrl+Y keeps the last instruction, and never names a direction — the steering tone is the only direction authority. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## ROL-26

- After an early vacate the handoff must never re-route to the PLANNED exit — with no runway edges in the graph, A* routes between two exits the long way round (KSEA: 1,678 m up the parallel taxiway and back). Retarget or conclude. "Early vacate" is ALONG-TRACK (`RolloutExitGate.IsVacateAwayFromPlannedExit`, 350 ft short) under a laterally-clear conjunct, never straight-line distance alone: the exit-node corridor runs just 5 m past the runway-clear boundary, so an aircraft off the pavement on its OWN exit is at most ~313 ft short of it. Claim NO measured spacing floor between distinct exits and never raise 350 toward one — `TaxiGraph`'s coverage-gap sweep measures the far ends of RET arcs, i.e. the SAME turnoff, and the one real datum on close distinct exits is the closest same-name pair kept, EGLL 09R S4E at 433 ft. A straight-line `TurnWindowFeet` test alone left the 350-1,000 ft band unguarded, so vacating onto a NEIGHBOURING exit re-routed to the planned one. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## ROL-27

- A landing-exit handoff route that RE-CROSSES the runway just landed on is refused at BOTH handoff sites, through the ONE `HandoffRouteReCrossesLandingRunway()` predicate so the two can never drift on which segments, cursor or runway they judge — the RESPONSES differ legitimately, the question does not, and a future third site must call it rather than re-derive it (`RolloutRunwayReCrossing` — the route classifier: refused when the route ENTERS or CROSSES the landing runway, never a per-edge intersection, which declined KORD 10R's W5 exit because its junction sits 29 cm over the centerline, and never a point-on-pavement test alone, which missed KBOS 33L; reciprocal-aware, so 26R and 08L are one pavement; judged from `_currentSegmentIndex` onward, because a crossing already behind the aircraft is history, not a route it is about to fly). It DECLINES only while the aircraft is still ON the runway with the exit AHEAD — `signedAlongPastFt < 0` AND `!offRunwayAtHandoff`, BOTH conjuncts, the second load-bearing rather than belt-and-braces: "keep rolling and the exit comes to you" is a claim about an aircraft on the pavement, and off it the overshoot detector cannot fire, so an `exitedLaterally` decline repeats at the retry floor forever with no closure and no route. Everything else CONCLUDES, and that closure splits on WHERE THE AIRCRAFT IS: two of the three landing-exit closures say "Stop and hold position", which is safe only off the pavement, so an on-runway conclude is steered to the "you may still be on the runway — continue ahead until clear" branch. Both sequences live on `ConcludeLandingExitOffRunway()` / `ConcludeLandingExitOnRunway()` (sharing `FinishLandingExitConclude`) — ONE owner for the reason split, the handoff-flag clear, the `Taxiing` transition and `HandleArrival`, which four guard sites previously hand-copied with nothing enforcing the identity this rule demands. `TryEarlyExitHandoff`'s decline returns FALSE and must restore `LandingRollout` ITSELF (`LoadRoute` left state at `RouteLoaded`) and discard the rejected route; its off-runway arm concludes and returns TRUE. The `UpdateLandingRollout` decline SPEAKS once (`ComposeContinueToExit` — names the exit AND the distance, never "stop"/"hold", never a claim of being clear; latched not floored, and re-armed by `RetargetLandingExit` when the targeted exit actually changes so a latch left over from the abandoned exit can't swallow the new one's callout): beyond `ExitToneArmFeet` a stopped aligned aircraft gets the turn-window `Silent` or a zero-volume `DriftCorrection`, and `trulyStopped` carries no distance gate, so without it a pilot who brakes short sits stationary and silent on an active runway. That sentence must be composed as ONE utterance with the rollout callouts it supersedes (`ComposeDeclineUtterance` + `DeclineSupersedesCallout`), never spoken beside them — the branch RETURNS before the approach/turn-now block, so their latches stay unset and the very next frame one of them `AnnounceImmediate`s over a one-shot sentence that can never be re-spoken; `speedNearExitHandoff` needs `distToExitFeet < ROLLOUT_NEAR_EXIT_FT` (500 ft) and the 500-ft milestone triggers on that same boundary, so the collision is structural, not a coincidence (sixth instance of this codebase's two-announcements-stomp-each-other pattern — the house remedy is one utterance). Every superseded APPROACH milestone is RETIRED, not folded (the sentence already names the exit with a LIVE distance); only their unique content rides along — the 500-ft cue's "Slow down." and the turn DIRECTION. The supersede window is a TIME converted at the aircraft's own ground speed, so it self-scales 0–90 kt and a stopped aircraft retires nothing ahead of it; turn-now deliberately uses the STRICT inside test instead, because "now" is time-critical. KATL 26R 2026-08-27: past B1 without turning, retargeted to exit A on the north side, and A* — with no runway edges to route along — built a 427 m loop out at B1, west along B and back north across the 08L threshold at 22 kt. `IsHandoffRouteReachable` cannot catch this: it measures only the FIRST segment's cross-track, and B1 started right at the aircraft. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## ROL-28

- `MatchEarlyVacateExit` measures along-track PER EXIT and must never compare against `DistanceFromThresholdFeet` — that is measured from the LANDING threshold and breaks at every displaced-threshold runway. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## ROL-29

- `IsHandoffRouteReachable` must gate every landing-exit handoff re-route — a route whose first segment the aircraft is not essentially already on must CONCLUDE guidance, never steer the tone at it (KSEA 34L: the tone panned 79° at a segment 53.9 m away with the aircraft 17.8 m outside the runway edge). → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## ROL-30

- **The rollout "turn now" trigger (`ROLLOUT_TURN_NOW_FT`, 150 ft) is GEOMETRIC and must stay so** — it also swings the Normal-exit tone onto `ExitBearingTrue`, so speed-scaling it hard-pans the tone hundreds of feet early at 40 kt, which is the failure above reintroduced through a different door. The LEAD is genuinely speed-dependent (rapid-exit guidance arms at 300 ft against this 150; the undershoot scan already uses `ROLLOUT_UNDERSHOOT_LEAD_PER_KT_FT` = 11 ft/kt ≈ 6.5 s; 150 ft is 4.4 s at 20 kt and 2.2 s at 40 kt), so the two jobs are SPLIT: a separate speed-scaled **preparatory** call on the same 11 ft/kt law fires first (`_rolloutTurnPrepAnnounced`, Normal exits only, capped at `ROLLOUT_TURN_PREP_MAX_FT` 400 ft so it can never land on the 500 ft milestone, skipped entirely when it would fall within `ROLLOUT_TURN_PREP_MIN_GAP_FT` of the turn point). Arrive fast → warned earlier, never pulled earlier. If the turn point reads late, move `ROLLOUT_TURN_NOW_FT` — which moves the verbal and the tone together — never scale it with speed. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## ROL-31

- **The Normal-exit speed handoff must wait for the turn-now callout, never fire at `nearExit` (500 ft).** `speedNearExitHandoff` is gated on `_rolloutTurnNowAnnounced`, because everything the KDTW 22L fix built lives in `UpdateLandingRollout` and dies the moment the state leaves `LandingRollout`: the Normal-exit tone that holds RUNWAY HEADING until the turn point, the speed-scaled prep call, and the 150 ft "Turn left now" verbal. On a normal decelerating landing the aircraft is below 30 kt a long way out, so the old gate fired dead-centre on the runway (HESH 04L, live 2026-08-26: hdgDelta 0.4°, lateral 36 ft, 480 ft short) and handed the pilot to `UpdatePosition`'s `_rolloutHandoffActive` branch, which snaps the tone to the exit SEGMENT bearing — a full 90° hard pan 152 m before the junction, held 18 s with no verbal at all. Following it, the turn began 443 ft early and cut the corner, passing 59 m from the junction node; the 25 m arrival radius still reported a clean vacate. Keying on the LATCH rather than a 150 ft distance test is required: this block runs BEFORE the turn-now callout in the same method and returns, so a distance gate swallows the verbal one frame later. `turnBegun` (15°), `trulyStopped` (<3 kt) and the in-rollout overshoot detector still cover every other exit path. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## ROL-32

- **Both post-handoff tone sources carry a bounded cross-track pull** (`exitCrossTrackPull`: 1°/m, ±20°, alongside the current segment, segment within 30° of the runway, never into a sharp turn or on a turn-back exit) — without it an aircraft parallel to a shallow exit but 16 m wide read zero error (KPDX 28R A6, KATL 08L B11). VirtualPilot: 53 off-pavement + 11 false misses fixed, 0 new. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md) (Split 2026-10 from one bullet, words unchanged: its detour-retry sentence is now RTE-30; the VirtualPilot figure covered both.)

## ROL-33

- **Never derive the spoken exit turn direction from the bearing AIRCRAFT→junction** — use `ResolveExitTurnDirection` (junction→`ApronNodeId` bearing, then `ExitBearingTrue`, both vs runway heading, each needing `EXIT_TURN_DIRECTION_MIN_DEG` = 10° to be spoken; below that the direction word is DROPPED — "Turn now, taxiway X" — never guessed). Where a scenery models the junction ON the centreline the old rule's sign is decided by the aircraft's own tracking error, not by the exit: 5 of KDTW 22L's 13 junctions sit within a metre of the centreline (Y3 0.4 m, R 0.1 m, V 0.4 m, Z7 0.7 m, Z5 0.9 m), so a LEFT exit announces "Turn right now" for any aircraft tracking a metre left of centre. A confident wrong side is far worse for a blind pilot than no side. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## ROL-34

- **The rollout tone must never pull while the correct action is "go straight"** — "follow the tone" is this app's one contract, and a tone that has to be ignored until a verbal arrives destroys it. On a **Normal** (right-angle) exit the pre-turn desired heading is RUNWAY HEADING, not bearing-to-junction, switching to `ExitBearingTrue` only once the turn-now callout fires (so the tone then means "how much turn is LEFT" and decays to silence as the pilot aligns). Bearing-to-a-point amplifies ANY lateral offset — the aircraft's or the node's — without bound as it closes: measured on KDTW 22L against a 15° full-pan, a 10 m offset reads 6° at 300 ft but 47° at 30 ft, a 25 m offset 16° → 70°, so the tone saturates hard-over from parallax in the last seconds before an 81° turn the pilot has not started. This is the behaviour the block already CLAIMED ("≈ runway heading when the junction is on the centreline → tone stays silent"), made true when neither the aircraft nor the node is exactly on the centreline. **High-speed and End exits keep bearing-to-junction** — on a RET the drift onto the arc IS the manoeuvre, and `ExitBearingTrue` at that range is the EIDW S5 premature-hard-pan failure. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## ROL-35

- **Neither missed-exit detector may call a miss while the aircraft is TURNING ONTO the chosen exit's own path** (`LandingExitPathFollow.HoldsOffMiss`, fed by `GetRolloutExitPath` = graph route junction → `LandingExitDestination.Resolve`). ALL gates are load-bearing: within 10 m of the path, the path stretch there ≥ 5° off the runway, and the aircraft turned ≥ 5° to the SAME side and within 20° of that stretch; bounded to margin + 400 ft. Both detectors judge from the RUNWAY (along-past, heading < 15°, post-handoff lateral < 30 ft), which cannot tell "rolled straight past" from a shallow exit: YPPH 21 C9 leaves at 14° and is 20 ft off the centreline 100 ft past its junction, so the pilot flying it 12° into the turn with the tone centred was told "Missed C9. Retargeting C11" (live 2026-09-18). NEVER make it position-only: many sceneries (EGLL, LFPG) draw exits ALONG the centreline for 60-75 m, and a position-only hold-off (measured, 500 airports) delayed 6,350 straight-roll miss calls, 1,390 of them far enough that the retarget skipped the next exit. The heading gate keeps a straight roll (runway heading) exactly as before. → CLAUDE.md

## ROL-36

- **Both missed-exit detectors also wait out the exit's on-centreline STUB** (`LandingExitPathFollow.OnAxisRunMetres` on the `GetRolloutExitPath` path, less any `TurnPointOffsetFeet`; capped 200 m; 6 m lateral): many sceneries draw the exit taxiway down the runway centreline first (KORD 27L M 80 m, ZSPD 35R B7 99 m, EGLL 27L N7 40 m after a 3 m jog), so the route tone says "continue" while the junction + 100 ft miss check said "Missed". Measured with `tools/VirtualPilot` (100 busiest airports): 996 → 194 false misses. The price, accepted: a straight roll past a stub exit is called later (median 59 m). The two refusals are load-bearing — a path that turns BACK down the runway WHILE STILL WITHIN 30 m OF THE CENTRELINE (`OnAxisHairpinLateralMetres`; KPHL 35 E4, a 160° hairpin) or comes back onto the centreline after leaving it (KSDF 29 F) returns 0, or those landings lose the miss that currently rescues them. The hairpin refusal is scoped to near the runway: unscoped, any exit that loops back once out on the apron (KSTL 30L V, YBBN 01L T1, CYYC 29 C, 50-80 m out) lost its whole stub and heard "Missed" 31 m past the junction (44 false misses). Do NOT widen the 6 m band: 7.5 m and 9 m were measured and each fixes offset-stub misses (OOMS 26R A runs 7 m off the centreline) only by calling 11-24 straight rolls "missed" > 200 m late — the two positions are indistinguishable. Re-run VirtualPilot and `compare_codes.py` before touching any constant. Both detectors also hold a miss off while the aircraft is within 15 m of the exit's own path (`StillOnExitPath`, bounded to 800 ft past the margin) — an owner ruling (2026-09-23) that a false "Missed" to a tone-following pilot outranks a later call to one rolling straight past: false misses 68→22, STRAIGHT_MISS_LATE 14→101 (median 224 m). Do not remove it to speed up straight-roll calls. Both detectors take ONE margin, `MissMarginFeet()` = Max(main's `RolloutExitGate.OvershootMarginFor` read at `DivergenceAngleDegrees`, the same rule read at `ExitAngleDegrees` + `OnAxisMissExtensionFeet()`), and pass that same margin to `HoldsOffMiss`/`StillOnExitPath` — measured from the junction, `HoldsOffMiss`'s 400 ft window was used up by a long stub before the exit curved (KPHX 07L E12, 2026-09-23). Never ADD the stub to main's divergence margin: both describe the same stub, and summed a straight roll past LTFM 34L A7A was called 202 m past the junction (153 m divergence, 117 m stub). → [virtual-pilot.md](../virtual-pilot.md)

## ROL-37

- `exitedLaterally`'s heading branch (≥ 8°) is bounded to `ROLLOUT_NEAR_EXIT_FT` (500 ft) unless the aircraft is on mapped taxi pavement (`IsOnTaxiPavement` — a shallow earlier taxiway taken deliberately keeps the old handoff) (drifting onto the grass short of the exit is main's `OffPavementAlert` + DriftCorrection tone, not a handoff).
